# Consultation Call Checklist

## Before the Call
- [ ] Review their business (website, Facebook, Google reviews)
- [ ] Prepare relevant examples from portfolio
- [ ] Have pricing sheet ready
- [ ] Test call technology (if video/online)
- [ ] Have notepad/document ready for notes

---

## During the Call (15-20 minutes)

### 1. Introduction (2 mins)
- [ ] Thank them for their time
- [ ] Brief intro: "I'm Ryan, based in Banbury, specialize in affordable websites for small businesses"
- [ ] Set expectations: "This should take about 15 minutes, I'll ask some questions, show you examples, and give you a quote"

### 2. Discovery Questions (5-7 mins)

**Business Understanding:**
- [ ] What do you do? (Let them explain their business)
- [ ] How long have you been operating?
- [ ] Who are your typical customers?
- [ ] How do customers currently find you?

**Current Situation:**
- [ ] Do you have a website now? (If yes: What do you like/dislike about it?)
- [ ] Are you on social media? (Facebook/Instagram?)
- [ ] How do you currently get enquiries? (Phone/Facebook/walk-ins?)

**Website Goals:**
- [ ] What's the main goal for the website? (More enquiries? Look professional? Showcase work? Online orders?)
- [ ] What do you want customers to do when they visit? (Call you? Fill out form? See portfolio?)
- [ ] Any websites you like the look of? (Competitors or any industry)

**Content & Features:**
- [ ] What pages do you think you'd need? (Prompt: Home, About, Services, Contact, Gallery?)
- [ ] Do you have photos of your work? (Logo? Team photos? Product photos?)
- [ ] Any specific features? (Booking system? Online payments? Contact forms? Google Maps?)

**Practical Considerations:**
- [ ] What's your timeline? (ASAP? Flexible? Specific launch date?)
- [ ] What's your budget roughly? (Don't assume - let them tell you)
- [ ] Who will update the website? (You? Them? Never?)

### 3. Presentation (3-5 mins)

**Show Examples:**
- [ ] Share screen/send links to portfolio
- [ ] Show T Morris Painting (tradesperson example)
- [ ] Show Special Moments (e-commerce example)
- [ ] Show one relevant to their business type if available

**Explain Your Process:**
- [ ] "Here's how I work..."
- [ ] Week 1: Design mockup based on your brand
- [ ] Week 2: Build the site
- [ ] Week 3: You review, I make changes, then launch
- [ ] After launch: Quick training + 30 days support

**Unique Selling Points:**
- [ ] Mobile-friendly (70% of customers search on mobile)
- [ ] SEO optimized for local search
- [ ] 30-day money-back guarantee
- [ ] Based in Banbury (local, easy to reach)
- [ ] Affordable (from £350 vs £1000+ from agencies)

### 4. Pricing & Quote (3-5 mins)

**Present Options Based on Their Needs:**
- [ ] Starter Package (£350): 1-3 pages, contact form, Google Maps
- [ ] Standard Package (£600): 5-6 pages, gallery, SEO, everything in Starter
- [ ] Premium Package (£900): Up to 10 pages, blog, advanced features

**Recommendation:**
- [ ] "Based on what you've told me, I'd recommend [Package] because..."
- [ ] Explain what's included in that package
- [ ] Mention payment options: Full upfront OR 50% deposit + 50% on completion

**Handle Budget Concerns:**
- [ ] If budget is tight: Payment plan option (50% now, 50% in 30 days)
- [ ] ROI framing: "If this brings you just 1-2 extra jobs per month, it pays for itself"
- [ ] Compare to agency prices: "Agencies charge £1000-3000 for the same thing"

### 5. Close & Next Steps (2-3 mins)

**Trial Close:**
- [ ] "Does this sound like what you're looking for?"
- [ ] "Any questions or concerns?"

**If Interested:**
- [ ] "Great! Here's what happens next..."
- [ ] I'll email you a proposal + simple agreement
- [ ] Invoice for 50% deposit (£[amount])
- [ ] Once paid, we'll schedule kick-off call
- [ ] I'll start on the design
- [ ] "When would you like to get started?"

**If "Need to Think":**
- [ ] "Of course! Take your time - big decision"
- [ ] "I'll email you a proposal anyway so you have everything in writing"
- [ ] "Any questions, just call or message anytime"
- [ ] "When would be a good time to follow up? Next week?"

**If "Too Expensive":**
- [ ] Explore budget: "What were you thinking budget-wise?"
- [ ] Offer payment plan: "I can do 50% now (£[X]), 50% in 30 days?"
- [ ] Downgrade package: "The Starter package at £350 might work better?"
- [ ] ROI reframe: "Think of it as £30/month over a year - less than a mobile phone contract"

**Always End With:**
- [ ] "I'll send you a follow-up email within the hour with everything we discussed"
- [ ] "Thanks so much for your time, [Name]"
- [ ] "Looking forward to working with you!" (if interested) OR "Speak soon!" (if thinking)

## After the Call (Within 1 Hour)

### Immediate Actions:
- [ ] Send follow-up email with quote (use template from outreach-templates.md)
- [ ] Update tracking spreadsheet with notes
- [ ] Set calendar reminder for follow-up (if they're thinking about it)
- [ ] Add to CRM/project management if proceeding

### Email Should Include:
- [ ] Thank you for their time
- [ ] Summary of what was discussed
- [ ] Package recommendation with pricing
- [ ] What's included
- [ ] Next steps
- [ ] Link to portfolio
- [ ] Your contact details

## Common Questions & Answers

### "How long will it take?"
**Answer:** "Typically 2-3 weeks from deposit to launch. Week 1 I create the design, Week 2 I build it, Week 3 you review and I make any changes. Then we launch!"

### "Can I update it myself?"
**Answer:** "Yes! I include a quick training video showing you how to update text and photos. It's super simple - no coding needed. Plus I'm always available if you need help."

### "What if I don't like it?"
**Answer:** "30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not happy within 30 days of launch, full refund - no questions asked. But that's never happened yet! 😊"

### "Do you do SEO?"
**Answer:** "Yes! All packages include basic SEO - I optimize your site for local searches like '[your service] Banbury'. Advanced SEO (ongoing work) is extra, but the basics are included."

### "Can you add [X feature]?"
**Answer:** "Absolutely! [If simple:] That's included in the [Package] package. [If complex:] That would be an add-on, probably around £[X]. Let me include that in the quote."

### "Do you do hosting?"
**Answer:** "I can arrange hosting for you (around £5-10/month through a provider), or if you prefer to manage it yourself that's fine too. Most clients have me handle it - one less thing to worry about!"

### "What happens after launch?"
**Answer:** "You get 30 days of free support - any tweaks, changes, questions. After that, I offer maintenance packages if you want ongoing support, or you can just call me as needed (charged hourly)."

### "Can you redesign my existing website?"
**Answer:** "Yes! Depending on how much of the old site we keep vs. rebuild, it might be similar pricing to a new site. Let me look at your current site and give you a proper quote."

### "Do you do mobile apps?"
**Answer:** "Not mobile apps, but all my websites are mobile-friendly - they work perfectly on phones, tablets, and computers. For most small businesses, that's all you need!"

## Red Flags During Call

**End call politely if:**
- [ ] They want a huge site for £350 (unrealistic expectations)
- [ ] They're rude or disrespectful
- [ ] They want to "test you" with a free site first
- [ ] They want payment on completion only (no deposit)
- [ ] They seem like they'll be difficult to work with

**Polite exit:** "Based on what you're looking for, I don't think I'm the right fit. I'd recommend [larger agency/freelance marketplace/specific alternative]. Best of luck with your project!"

## Qualification Checklist

**Good Fit Indicators:**
✓ Clear business need (want more enquiries/look professional)
✓ Realistic budget (£350-900 range)
✓ Reasonable timeline (2-4 weeks)
✓ Have basic content (photos, logo, business info)
✓ Friendly and professional
✓ Local to Banbury/Bicester/Brackley area
✓ Small business (1-10 employees)

**Poor Fit Indicators:**
❌ Want £3000 website for £350
❌ Need it "by tomorrow"
❌ No budget at all
❌ Want to "think about it for 6 months"
❌ Rude or demanding
❌ Far outside your service area
❌ Large company (should use an agency)
