Frequently Asked Questions

Do I own the website after I pay?

Yes, absolutely! Once you pay, the website is yours. All the design, code, and content belong to you completely. I'll set everything up for you, including hosting, so you don't need to worry about the technical details.

What if I need changes later?

Within the first 30 days, unlimited revisions are included. After that, I charge £50/hour for updates, or you can hire another developer since you own the code.

How long does it take to build?

Bronze sites take about 2 weeks, Silver packages take 3 weeks, and Gold projects vary. Timelines depend on how quickly you provide content and feedback.

Do I need to provide content and images?

Yes, you'll need to provide your text content and photos. Don't have professional photos? I can use stock images as placeholders or recommend affordable photographers.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

I offer unlimited revisions within 30 days of launch. If you're still not satisfied, I'll refund you in full. Your success is my priority.

Can you help with SEO and Google rankings?

Every site is built with SEO best practices (proper meta tags, fast loading, mobile-friendly). For ongoing SEO work like content strategy or link building, I can recommend specialists or discuss a custom plan.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer is preferred. 50% deposit to start, 50% on completion. For larger projects, we can discuss payment milestones.
