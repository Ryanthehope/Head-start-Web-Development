## Objection Handling Templates

### "I'm not tech-savvy"
```
That's actually perfect! My sites are designed to be super simple. You won't need to touch any code or technical stuff.

I set everything up for you, and if you ever want to update text or photos, I include a quick training video (5 mins). Plus I'm always available for questions.

Most of my clients are [tradespeople/business owners] who just want something that works - no tech knowledge needed! 👍
```

### "I already have Facebook"
```
Totally get it - Facebook is great! But here's the thing:

1. When people Google "[your service] near me", a website shows up - not Facebook
2. A website looks more professional when you're quoting jobs
3. You can share your website link in texts, emails, business cards
4. You own it - can't get "locked out" like social media

Think of it as: Facebook for your existing customers, website for Google search and new customers finding you 👍
```

### "Can you do it cheaper?"
```
I keep prices low by being a sole trader (no office costs or sales team to pay for!).

The £350 package is honestly as low as I can go while delivering quality work. But I do offer payment plans - £175 x 2 months if that helps?

Also, 30-day money-back guarantee - so zero risk. If you don't love it, full refund 👍
```

### "I'll think about it"
```
Of course! Big decision, take your time 👍

Would it help if I sent over some examples of sites I've built for other [tradespeople/salons/cafes]? Sometimes it's easier to decide when you can see what's possible.

Also happy to answer any questions - no pressure at all!
```

### "I need to discuss with my partner/business partner"
```
Absolutely - smart move to discuss big decisions together! 👍

Would it help if I put together a quick overview you can share with them? I can include examples, pricing, what's included - makes it easier to discuss.

Or happy to jump on a quick call with both of you if that's easier?
```

## Tips for Using These Templates

### Personalization Checklist:
- [ ] Use their actual name
- [ ] Mention something specific about their business
- [ ] Reference their location if relevant
- [ ] Adjust tone to match their business (formal for established businesses, casual for sole traders)
- [ ] Include relevant emoji for Facebook/WhatsApp (but not email)

### Best Practices:
✓ Respond within 1 hour during business hours (9am-6pm)
✓ Keep messages short and scannable
✓ Always include a clear next step/question
✓ Use their language (if they say "website", don't switch to "digital presence")
✓ Mirror their communication style (formal/casual, emoji/no emoji)

### Red Flags to Avoid:
❌ Generic copy-paste with no personalization
❌ Pushing for immediate decision
❌ More than 2 follow-ups if no response
❌ Sending messages late at night (looks unprofessional)
❌ Using technical jargon
