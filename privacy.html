Privacy and Cookies

Head-Start Web Development respects your privacy. This page explains what information this website collects and why.

Contact enquiries

If you use the contact form, the information you provide is sent through Formspree and used to respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote. Please only include information needed to discuss your project.

You can also contact Head-Start Web Development directly at headstartwebdevelopment@gmail.com or 07754 858861.

Analytics cookies

Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after you choose Accept on the cookie banner. It helps us understand visits and improve the website. Analytics is configured with anonymised IP processing.

Your cookie choice is stored in this browser using local storage. Choosing Reject prevents Google Analytics from loading. You can clear this choice at any time by clearing this site's stored data in your browser.

Third-party services

This website uses Google Fonts, Google Analytics after consent, and Formspree for contact-form delivery. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can choose whether optional Analytics cookies load. You can also contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you or to request correction or deletion where applicable.

Contact

For privacy questions, email headstartwebdevelopment@gmail.com.
