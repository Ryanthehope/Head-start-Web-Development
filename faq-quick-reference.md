# FAQ Quick Reference

## Quick Answers for Common Questions

### Pricing Questions

**"How much does a website cost?"**
From £350 for a simple site (1-3 pages), £600 for standard (5-6 pages), up to £900 for premium (10 pages with blog). What are you looking for?

**"Why so cheap compared to agencies?"**
I'm a sole trader with no office rent or sales team to pay for! I keep costs low so small businesses can afford quality websites. Plus I'm building my portfolio while studying.

**"Can I pay monthly?"**
I don't offer monthly payment plans, but I can split it into 2 payments: 50% deposit, 50% on completion. Or 50% now, 50% in 30 days.

**"Do you offer payment plans?"**
50% deposit to start, 50% on completion. Or if budget is tight, 50% now and 50% in 30 days. That work for you?

**"Is hosting included?"**
Hosting is separate (around £5-10/month), but I can arrange it for you or you can sort it yourself. Most clients have me handle everything.

**"What about domain name?"**
Domain (yourname.co.uk) is separate - about £10-15/year. I can register it for you or you can buy it yourself.

### Objections

**"I already have a Facebook page"**
Facebook is great! But 64% of people Google "[service] near me" before booking. A website = you show up in Google. Plus websites look professional on quotes/cards.

**"I'm not tech-savvy"**
Perfect! My sites are built for non-tech people. You won't touch any code. Quick training video shows you how to update basic stuff. I'm always here to help.

**"I don't have time"**
We only need about 2-3 hours of your time total: 1 hour initial chat, 30 mins review draft, 30 mins final review. We do all the heavy lifting!

**"I'll do it myself on Wix"**
You could! But that's 20-30 hours of learning + building. Is your time worth saving £350? Plus custom sites look more professional than DIY ones.

**"Let me think about it"**
Of course! Big decision. What specific concerns do you have? [Address them]. When would be good time to follow up? Next week?

**"I need to ask my wife/partner"**
Smart! Would it help if I sent over examples and pricing for you to show them? Or happy to chat with both of you on a call if easier?

**"Business is slow right now"**
I hear you. But that's exactly when to invest in marketing! Website works 24/7 bringing in enquiries even when you're not. Help turn things around?

**"I'll wait until I'm busier"**
Catch-22! You need the website to get busier. Think of it like buying a van - you need it to do more jobs. Website brings in the work.

## Helpful Analogies

**"Website = Digital Shopfront"**
You wouldn't open a physical shop without a sign, right? Website is your digital shopfront on Google - people searching find you 24/7.

**"Website = Business Card on Steroids"**
Business card gets lost. Website is always there, shows your work, let's people contact you, works while you sleep.

**"£350 = Cost of 1-2 Jobs"**
For most tradespeople, £350 is 1-2 jobs. If the website brings you just ONE extra job per month, it pays for itself in 1-2 months.

**"£30/month"**
Break it down: £350 over a year = £30/month. Less than a phone contract, and it's a one-time payment (no monthly fees).

## Tone Guidelines

**Be:**
✓ Friendly and approachable (not corporate)
✓ Confident but humble  
✓ Clear and jargon-free
✓ Helpful (even if they don't buy)
✓ Local ("fellow Banbury business")

**Don't Be:**
❌ Pushy or salesy
❌ Defensive about price
❌ Technical (avoid jargon)
❌ Desperate (you're doing them a favor too!)
❌ Negative about competitors

## When to Walk Away

**Politely decline if:**
- Budget is unrealistic (want £3000 site for £200)
- Timeline is impossible (need it tomorrow)
- They're rude or disrespectful
- Want everything for free first
- Give you bad vibes (trust your gut)
- Scope is way beyond your skills

**Polite decline script:**
"Thanks for thinking of me! Based on what you need, I don't think I'm the right fit. I'd recommend [alternative - agency for big projects, Fiverr for tiny budgets, etc.]. Best of luck with your project!"
